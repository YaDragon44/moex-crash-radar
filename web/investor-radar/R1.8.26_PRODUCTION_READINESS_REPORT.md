## SBER valuation blocker closure

The previous R1.8.36 HOLD was based on the assumption that common BVPS/P-B required a reconstructed allocation of total equity between ordinary and preferred shares. R1.8.44 introduced primary issuer evidence for **net assets per ordinary/common share**, and R1.8.46 explicitly accepts independently verified issuer-reported common-share BVPS as a valid denominator source.

Therefore the old requirements for preferred liquidation/equity priority and an equity-allocation method are no longer blocking when issuer-reported common-share BVPS is independently verified. Total capital and regulatory capital remain forbidden substitutes for common equity.
