## Investment logic review

The validated signal is an early risk-transition / EXIT warning, not a prediction of a crash date or price target. A confirmed EXIT event must not be interpreted as an automatic `SELL ALL` instruction. The observed false-alarm rate remains material, so capital actions should be graduated and later combined with independent Market Risk / Positioning / Transition layers.

The current validation does **not** validate Crowd State, Market Regime, Re-entry, Bull/Bubble scores or a statistical probability of a market decline.

## Known limitations

- Blind sample is still small: four completed forward-horizon EXIT events.
- The 2026-08-17 event cannot yet be classified because its 20-row forward horizon is incomplete.
- Precision is event-based under the predefined <= -8% / 20-row outcome rule; it is not a probability estimate.
- Recall on the blind window is not promoted as a production metric until an independent ex-ante crash-event catalogue is frozen. Using ex-post drawdowns to manufacture event labels after seeing model outputs would contaminate the blind test.
- Risk Reduction requires a separately frozen capital-action policy; it is not inferred from EXIT detection alone.
