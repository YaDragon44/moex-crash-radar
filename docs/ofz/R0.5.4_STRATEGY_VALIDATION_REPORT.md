2. It is not a hedge against an initial rise in long OFZ yields because A/B/C all keep 100% exposure.

4. In a fall-then-reversal path, the rule improves drawdown but at a meaningful terminal-return cost.
5. Turnover can become very high; FALL_REVERSAL produced active turnover above 3.25x initial capital before commissions/slippage.
