Conclusion: current policy behaves as post-rally profit locking, not a universal duration hedge.

### R0.5.7 Real common-life MOEX backtest
Common real interval: 2025-11-27 to 2026-09-11, 200 common trading days, 12 monthly observations.
Observed CBR regimes: A/B only.
All policies equal Buy & Hold because all candidate policies specify 100% exposure in A/B.
Result: real MOEX mechanics are validated, but D/E thresholds remain untested by the current basket's realized common-life history.

## Decision logic
A production threshold change requires evidence that:
1. the candidate improves the intended objective relative to Buy & Hold/current policy;
2. the test includes the regimes where the candidate differs (D/E);
3. the result is based on sufficiently representative market data;
4. the improvement is large enough to justify extra turnover and model risk.

Current evidence fails condition 2 for realized common-life MOEX data and does not establish a unique winner under proxy/scenario tests.

Therefore changing 100/60/25 now would be overfitting.

## Formal decision
KEEP CURRENT PRODUCTION POLICY: 100/100/100/60/25.
Confidence: MEDIUM.
Reason for not upgrading confidence: D/E behavior has no real-price common-life validation for the exact basket.
