## Interpretation
This equality is expected, not a simulation failure. The common-life CBR 10Y observations are entirely in regimes A/B. All candidate policies specify 100% exposure in A/B. Therefore no policy has a reason to rebalance away from BUY & HOLD during this interval.

R0.5.7 validates the realized MOEX price/coupon path mechanics, but it cannot discriminate the D/E de-risking thresholds because the common-life sample contains no D/E observations.
